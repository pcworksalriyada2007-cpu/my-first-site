About the Lion

The lion is a large wild cat known as the "king of the jungle."

Lions live in groups called prides.

They live in Africa and eat meat.

Quick Facts

  • Diet: Meat
  • Habitat: Savannas and grasslands of Africa
  • Size: Males can weigh up to 420 kg (926 lbs)
Scientific Name:
Panthera leo
Life Span:
10-14 years in the wild
